

## ✨ Features

Tajiri AI is a smart personal finance assistant designed to help you track, understand, and improve your financial habits through conversation and proactive insights.

###  Core Financial Tracking

* **📊 Transaction Management**: Easily log your expenses, income, and account-to-account transfers.
* **🏦 Multi-Account Support**: Manage multiple financial accounts, each with its own currency and balance.
* **🎨 Customizable Categories**: Create your own spending and income categories with unique names, colors, and icons for personalized tracking.
* **🏠 Dashboard Overview**: A central dashboard gives you an at-a-glance view of your total balance, recent transactions, and overall financial health.

### 🤖 AI-Powered Intelligence

* **💬 Conversational AI Advisor**: Chat with Tajiri, your AI assistant, in natural language. Ask financial questions or tell it to log transactions for you.
* **🧠 Smart Intent Recognition**: The AI can understand your intent from a simple sentence. For example, saying *"I spent 5,000 on lunch"* will automatically prepare a transaction for you to confirm.
* **📈 Spending Pattern Alerts**: The AI proactively analyzes your spending and notifies you of unusual spikes in any category compared to your historical average.
* **💡 Personalized Nudges**: Receive gentle reminders to engage with the AI advisor if you have questions, helping you make the most of the app.

### 🎯 Budgeting & Savings Goals

* **💰 Monthly Budgeting**: Set monthly spending limits for any of your custom categories to stay on track.
* **🏆 Goal Setting & Tracking**: Create personal savings goals with a target amount and deadline. Tajiri automatically calculates your progress daily based on your spending habits.

### 🔔 Proactive & Motivational Notifications

* **🎉 Milestone Celebrations**: Get celebratory notifications when you hit major progress points on your savings goals (e.g., 25%, 50%, 100%).
* **🔥 Habit-Building Streaks**: Stay motivated with notifications that celebrate consistent expense logging (e.g., a 7-day or 30-day streak).
* **⚠️ Smart Alerts**: Receive local, offline-capable notifications if a transaction causes you to get close to or exceed your budget limit for a category.
* **⏰ Daily Reminders**: Get a friendly, non-intrusive reminder each evening to log your day's expenses, with varied messages to keep it fresh.

### ⚙️ Customization & User Experience

* **🌓 Light & Dark Themes**: Choose the look and feel that's right for you.
* **✈️ Offline Support**: Log transactions even when you're offline. Your data will be securely saved on your device and synced with the cloud once you reconnect.
* **🔐 Secure Authentication**: Your financial data is protected behind user authentication.
